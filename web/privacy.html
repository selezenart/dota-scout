Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Captain’s Draft Board is a free, independent drafting tool for Dota 2 teams, run by the Captain’s Draft Board team. This page explains what data the site stores, why, and who can see it.

What we store

You can use local practice without an account. Nothing you enter there leaves your browser.

When you create an account or sign in, we store:

  • Account details. For email sign-in: your email address and a password, which only our sign-in provider can check. For Google sign-in: the email address and name Google shares with us. For Steam sign-in: your Steam account ID. We never see your Steam or Google password.
  • Your player profile, as you fill it in: display name, Dota 2 account ID, preferred roles, signature and wanted heroes, heroes to avoid, availability, notes and profile hero.
  • Team data: teams you create or join, members and invite links, team names and emblem images suggested by members, shared drafts, Battle Cup signups and scouting notes (opponents’ public Dota 2 account IDs, match IDs and which heroes they played).

Who can see it

  • Your profile and team data are visible to the members of your teams.
  • The open Battle Cup lineup shows your display name and chosen positions to other signed-in players.
  • Team emblem image links can be opened by anyone who has the link.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

Data kept in your browser

The site saves practice drafts, scouting notes, sign-in state and pending invitations in your browser’s local storage so they survive a reload. You can clear them by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and team data and handles sign-in.
  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time.
  • Steam and Google confirm your identity when you choose those sign-in options. Hero images load from Valve’s Steam CDN.
  • OpenDota and STRATZ provide public match statistics. Your browser asks OpenDota for the public stats of the Dota 2 account IDs you enter.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account exists. A captain can delete a team, which removes its drafts, signups, scouting notes and members. Members can leave a team at any time.

To delete your account and profile, or to ask what we store about you, open an issue at github.com/selezenart/dota-scout/issues. We will delete your account and profile and confirm when it is done.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
